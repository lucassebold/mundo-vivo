A colony of digital bacteria, live

Every digital bacterium has an artificial brain and lives in the same culture medium, running 24/7 on a server. At the start they knew nothing; the ones that absorb nutrients divide into daughter cells with similar brains, and evolution happens on its own. The purple shapes are amoebas, which hunt bacteria. We follow one hero cell. In about 15 minutes it grows through six levels until it becomes an AI Agent that can talk with you in the chat. The Agent lives only a few minutes; then it fades, and a new cycle begins with a new hero. The names are real genera, but the behavior is a simplified simulation.
