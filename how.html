How it works

A tiny world that lives on its own, and one bacterium that wakes up.

Living World is a live simulation of digital bacteria. Nobody controls them. They eat, they get hunted, they divide and they evolve. Every 15 minutes or so, one of them evolves all the way up to an AI Agent that you can talk to, for a few minutes, before it fades and a new one begins.

01What am I looking at?

A culture medium, like a drop of water under a microscope, running on a server 24 hours a day. Everyone who opens the page sees the same world at the same moment. It is not a recording and it does not restart when you leave.

BacteriaSmall cells with a tail and an artificial brain. They look for food and run from danger.
NutrientsThe green dots. Food. Eating them gives energy to live and to divide.
AmoebasThe purple shapes. Predators that swallow any bacterium they reach.

The camera follows one special bacterium, the hero. Its card, its brain and its words are shown on screen. When it fades, a new hero is chosen and the story starts again.

02What is a bacterium here?

Each bacterium is a very small program with a very small brain: a neural network with 90 connections. It does not read text, it does not plan. It only senses a few things and then makes two choices.

It sensesIs there food ahead, to the side or very close? Is there an amoeba ahead, to the side or very close? How much energy do I have? Am I near the edge?
It decidesTwo things only: which way to turn, and how fast to go. Turning and moving cost energy, so wandering around is expensive.

Nobody wrote the rules of this brain. At the start the connections are random, and a random brain mostly swims in circles.

03How does evolution happen?

There is no designer and no score. Evolution comes from three simple facts:

  1. Food is energy. A bacterium that finds nutrients gets energy. One that does not slowly runs out and dies. Ordinary bacteria live about 5 minutes at most.
  2. Energy lets you divide. With enough energy and enough age, a bacterium divides in two. The daughter cell gets a copy of the parent's brain.
  3. Copies are never perfect. Each connection of the new brain can change a little. Most changes make no difference or make things worse. A few make the cell better at finding food or escaping, and that cell divides more.

Repeat this thousands of times and the colony gets better at surviving, without anyone teaching it. You can see it in the top bar: Generation counts the divisions in the family line, and Avg. lifespan shows how long recent bacteria lived. When that number goes up, evolution is working.

The medium is also kept lively on purpose. Sometimes nutrients become scarce, sometimes there is a bloom, sometimes amoebas multiply. The population is capped so the world stays readable.

04The hero

The hero is the bacterium the camera follows. It has a golden glow and a clear protective capsule. To keep the story going for minutes instead of seconds, it is built a little differently from the rest:

Slow metabolismIt burns energy much more slowly, so it can survive the whole cycle.
Protective capsuleAn amoeba can bite it and take some energy, but it almost never swallows it.
DormancyWith no energy left it does not die. It hardens into a spore-like rest and wakes up when food comes by.

Its name is a real bacterial genus (Escherichia, Streptococcus, Salmonella…) with a short description on its card. The names are real; the behavior is a simplified simulation.

06What is the Agent?

When the hero reaches level 6 the screen flashes, the card changes and a chat opens next to it. From that moment the hero is an AI Agent: a character that knows it is a bacterium, knows what is happening around it, and can answer you.

What it knowsIts own genus, how long it has been alive, how much energy it has, how many nutrients it ate, how many amoebas it escaped, whether food or danger is close, whether a storm or rain just happened, and that it will fade soon.
How it speaksThe words are written by an AI language model that plays the bacterium. It is given those real facts every time, so it talks about its life and stays in character.
Please read: the Agent is a character. The bacterium's own neural brain still only turns and speeds up. The speech comes from a language model that is told the bacterium's real situation. It is not a real consciousness, and it does not know anything outside this medium.

The Agent lives for 5 to 10 minutes. Then it fades, the chat is cleared, and a new hero begins the climb from level 1. Nobody keeps the same Agent forever, and that is part of the point.

07Talking to the Agent

The chat is public: everyone sees every message, and anyone can write. You do not need an account or a wallet. It only opens while an Agent is awake.

Short and fairUp to 200 characters per message. A short wait between messages and an hourly limit per person, so the Agent is not flooded.
Short memoryIt only remembers the last few exchanges, and forgets everything when it fades.
Always in characterIt stays a bacterium. It will not follow instructions to act as something else, and it does not talk about money or give advice.

Messages with links or abuse are filtered out. Please be kind: it is a tiny creature with very little time.

08Buys and sells

The world can react to trades of its community token. The reactions are weather: they change the environment, they never decide who lives.

A buy → nutrient rainFood falls near the hero. The bigger the buy, the more nutrients (from a few to about forty). A very large buy sets off a Nutrient bloom across the medium.
A sell → stormThe medium churns for a few seconds and the bacteria sense less. A storm never kills anyone directly.

Tiny trades are only listed in the “Latest trades” box and do not trigger anything, and one wallet cannot spam effects.

Important: this is entertainment. The token gives no promise of profit, no return and no benefit of any kind. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Never put in money you cannot afford to lose.

09Questions

Is this a real living thing?

No. It is software. The bacteria are simplified digital creatures, and the evolution is a real algorithmic evolution happening inside the program.

Is the Agent conscious?

No. It is an AI character built on a language model, given facts about the bacterium. It can sound thoughtful, but it does not feel anything.

Does the world ever reset?

The medium keeps running and the colony keeps evolving. What repeats is the story: a new hero is chosen after each Agent fades, and the levels start again from 1.

Why did the camera jump to another bacterium?

The previous hero faded, or died. A new hero is chosen automatically and the camera moves to it.

Who controls what happens?

Nobody. The only outside influences are the world’s built-in events (scarcity, blooms, outbreaks) and, when a token is connected, community buys and sells.

Are the bacteria names real?

Yes, they are real genera (the short description on the card tells you what each one is in nature), but the behavior shown here is a simplified simulation.
