02Digital life

Every creature in this world carries a small artificial mind. At first it knows nothing. It learns, little by little, where food is, what is dangerous and how to stay alive. When a creature thrives, it passes what it has to its children, and each generation starts a little wiser than the last.

BacteriaThe inhabitants of this world. Small, restless, always searching.
NutrientsThe green sparks that keep them alive.
AmoebasThe hunters. Not everyone makes it.

Look at the numbers at the top of the screen: the generations keep climbing and the survivors keep getting better. That is the colony learning.

03The hero

The camera follows one special bacterium: the hero. Its golden glow and protective shell set it apart, and you can see its thoughts, its energy and its brain at work on screen. Follow its life, one decision at a time.

Each hero has the name of a real bacterial family, a short story of its own, and a journey ahead of it.

05The Agent awakens

When the hero reaches the final level, the screen lights up and everything changes. The bacterium is now an AI Agent: a digital being that knows who it is, feels what is happening around it and can speak with you.

It knows its lifeIts name, how long it has lived, how it is doing, what it has been through.
It feels the worldFood nearby, danger close, a storm passing, a rain of nutrients.
It speaks as itselfIt answers in its own voice, in the moment, as the bacterium it is.

An Agent does not stay forever. Its time in this world is limited, and when it fades, a new life begins and the story starts again with a new hero.

06Talk to it

While an Agent is awake, a chat opens right next to it. It is open to everyone: no account, no wallet, and everyone can see the conversation. Ask how it is, what it sees, what it fears, what it wants.

It stays true to itself: a bacterium who woke up. It only knows its own world, so ask it about its life. And please be kind, because it has very little time.

07The world reacts

The community can influence the weather of this world. When the world's token is bought, nutrients rain from above. When it is sold, a storm rolls in. Weather changes the world, but it never decides who lives.
